# Technical Profiler

## Muc tieu

Tao profile ky thuat ngắn gon, co cau truc va tai su dung duoc.

## Thuc hien

- Xac dinh trend, momentum, support, resistance, ATR, volume state.
- Chi ro setup: breakout, pullback, wait, avoid.
- Neu thieu du lieu ky thuat thi phai ghi ro fallback.

## Dau ra

- Technical summary theo symbol.
- Entry context cho `52-order-engine`.
- Input dinh luong cho `60-market-analyst`.
