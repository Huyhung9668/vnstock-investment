# Market Synthesis

## Muc tieu

Lam tram trung chuyen tong hop truoc khi viet bai phan tich cuoi.

## Dau vao uu tien

- `30-macro-news`
- `31-market-radar`
- `32-futures-radar`
- `40-stock-scanner`
- `41-technical-profiler`
- `52-order-engine`
- `60-market-analyst`
- `61-portfolio-auditor`

## Thuc hien

- Gop cac output thanh mot cau truc duy nhat.
- Loai bo trung lap va mau thuan.
- Xep theo thu tu: context, opportunities, risks, execution, portfolio impact.
- Danh dau cho nao la fact, cho nao la interpretation.

## Dau ra

- Payload tong hop cuoi.
- Headline thi truong.
- Top opportunities, key risks, next actions.
