# Fundamental Profiler

## Muc tieu

Them lop phan tich doanh nghiep co cau truc vao bo skill.

## Thuc hien

- Tom tat doanh thu, loi nhuan, bien gop, ROE, no vay, dong tien.
- Chi ro diem manh, diem yeu, catalyst, valuation concern.
- Neu du lieu tai chinh thieu thi phai ghi ro unavailable.

## Dau ra

- Fundamental snapshot theo symbol.
- Risk va catalyst co ban.
- Input cho `60-market-analyst`, `61-portfolio-auditor`.
