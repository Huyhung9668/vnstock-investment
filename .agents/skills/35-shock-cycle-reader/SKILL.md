# Shock Cycle Reader

## Muc tieu

Doc chu ky tam ly sau tin xau hoac bien dong manh de biet thi truong dang o pha nao: soc, hoang loan, rut cung, hay tai tich luy.

## Thuc hien

- Tong hop bien dong gia, do rong, thanh khoan, va cac moc su kien gan day.
- Xac dinh thi truong dang o pha panic that hay chi la rung lac trong xu huong lon hon.
- Neu co dau hieu rut cung, neu ro dieu kien xac nhan va dieu kien that bai.
- Viet thanh thong diep ngan, de hieu, tranh thuat ngu kho doc.

## Mau dien giai

- Tin xau manh thuong tao ra pha giai toa tam ly truoc khi thi truong tim diem can bang moi.
- Neu luc giam di kem thanh khoan cao dot bien va sau do ap luc ban giam dan, day co the la dau hieu rut cung.
- Chua nen vọi mua neu do rong van yeu va dong tien chua quay lai nhom dan dat.

## Dau ra

- Shock-cycle narrative.
- Nhan dinh tam ly thi truong theo pha.
- Input cho `30-macro-news`, `31-market-radar`, `60-market-analyst`, `73-telegram-market-brief`.
