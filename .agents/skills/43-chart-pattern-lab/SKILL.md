# Chart Pattern Lab

## Muc tieu

Bo sung lop hinh anh va pattern visualization cho trading terminal.

## Thuc hien

- Lay input tu du lieu gia da chuan hoa.
- Ve chart ro rang, uu tien de doc nhanh.
- Danh dau vung quan trong: support, resistance, breakout, stop invalidation.
- Khong ket luan pattern neu chart qua thieu du lieu.

## Tai nguyen

- Doc `references/chart-guidelines.md` khi can quy tac trinh bay.

## Dau ra

- Chart PNG/HTML.
- Pattern notes ngan gon.
- Input cho `62-professional-pdf` va `63-dashboard-publisher`.
