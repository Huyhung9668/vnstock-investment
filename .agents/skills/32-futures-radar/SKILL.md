# Futures Radar

## Muc tieu

Bo sung goc nhin ngan han tu thi truong phai sinh vao boi canh tong the.

## Thuc hien

- Theo doi basis, spread, dong luc intraday, thanh khoan, open interest neu co.
- So sanh phai sinh voi thi truong co so.
- Chi ro dau hieu hedging, panic, squeeze hay risk appetite.

## Dau ra

- Short-term sentiment note.
- Canh bao sai lech giua futures va spot.
- Input cho `31-market-radar` va `70-market-synthesis`.
