# Professional PDF

## Muc tieu

Bien bai phan tich da tong hop thanh ban trinh bay chuyen nghiep.

## Thuc hien

- Giữ cau truc heading, summary, charts, key risks, action plan.
- Uu tien Markdown/HTML truoc, PDF sau.
- Khong them noi dung moi neu khong co trong payload tong hop.

## Dau ra

- Markdown report sach.
- HTML/PDF san sang chia se.
- Ban institutional-style report neu du lieu da on dinh.
